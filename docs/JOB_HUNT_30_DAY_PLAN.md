# 30-Day Job Hunt Plan (Frontend/Product Engineer)

## Goal

Land interviews (and offers) for **remote frontend/product engineering roles** using your Stanect project as a flagship case study.

---

## North-Star Targets (30 days)

- **Applications sent:** 80–120 high-quality applications
- **Referrals requested:** 25–40
- **Recruiter/outreach messages:** 80+
- **Portfolio updates shipped:** 3
- **Mock interviews completed:** 6–10
- **Interview conversion target:** 8–15 first-round interviews

---

## Weekly Breakdown

## Week 1 — Positioning + Assets

### Primary Objective

Package your profile so recruiters can immediately understand your value.

### Deliverables

- Finalize portfolio case study for Stanect
- Update CV/resume with impact bullets
- Optimize LinkedIn headline/about/featured section
- Prepare 3 project talking scripts (2-minute + 5-minute versions)

### Output Targets

- 15 applications
- 10 outreach messages
- 5 referral asks

### Checklist

- [ ] Resume has quantified outcomes (or clear metric placeholders)
- [ ] LinkedIn headline reflects role target (e.g., Frontend/Product Engineer)
- [ ] GitHub profile pinned projects are updated
- [ ] Portfolio includes architecture + business impact sections

---

## Week 2 — Volume + Referrals

### Primary Objective

Increase top-of-funnel while maintaining quality.

### Deliverables

- Apply consistently across remote boards and company career pages
- Prioritize companies using your stack (Next.js, TS, React Query, Zustand)
- Start referral campaign through alumni, communities, LinkedIn mutuals

### Output Targets

- 25–30 applications
- 20 outreach messages
- 10 referral asks
- 2 recruiter calls booked

### Messaging Focus

- “Product-minded frontend engineer”
- End-to-end execution (UX + architecture + reliability)
- Clear ownership from problem to shipped outcome

---

## Week 3 — Interview Readiness + Iteration

### Primary Objective

Convert responses into interviews with better storytelling.

### Deliverables

- 4 technical mock interviews
- 2 behavioral mocks (STAR)
- Refine answers based on interviewer feedback
- Add one mini case-study visual (before/after screenshots)

### Output Targets

- 25–30 applications
- 25 outreach messages
- 10 referral asks
- 4+ interview rounds scheduled

### Interview Prep Topics

- Architecture decisions and trade-offs
- State management strategy
- Validation across boundaries
- Production hardening/failure handling
- Performance and maintainability refactors

---

## Week 4 — Offer Pipeline + Negotiation

### Primary Objective

Push active pipelines to final rounds and prepare negotiation.

### Deliverables

- Final-round prep packets per company
- Compensation research by role/location policy
- Offer comparison sheet (salary, equity, growth, scope)

### Output Targets

- 20–25 applications
- 25 outreach messages
- 10 referral asks
- 2–5 final rounds / offer-stage conversations

### Negotiation Prep

- Desired range (ideal / acceptable / floor)
- Value narrative tied to product outcomes
- Clear questions on level expectations and growth path

---

## Daily Execution Template (Mon–Fri)

- **60 min:** 4–6 targeted applications
- **30 min:** 4 outreach/referral messages
- **30 min:** Interview prep (system/product/behavioral)
- **20 min:** Portfolio/LinkedIn optimization
- **10 min:** Pipeline tracking update

Total: ~2.5 hours/day (increase on weekends if possible)

---

## Application Quality Standard

Before sending, confirm:

- [ ] CV tailored to role keywords
- [ ] 2–3 bullets map directly to job description
- [ ] Cover note references specific company problem/use case
- [ ] Portfolio link points to relevant case study
- [ ] Stack alignment is obvious in first 15 seconds

## KPI Tracker (Copy into Notion/Sheet)

- Applications sent
- Responses received
- Recruiter screens
- Technical interviews
- Final rounds
- Rejections (with reason)
- Referrals requested
- Referrals converted
- Offer-stage count

Track weekly conversion rates:

- Response rate = responses / applications
- Interview rate = interviews / responses
- Final-round rate = finals / interviews

---

## If Response Rate Is Low (After 2 Weeks)

Do this immediately:

1. Rewrite resume headline and top 3 bullets for clarity + impact
2. Add one quantified metric per major project section
3. Tighten portfolio page above-the-fold (role, stack, outcomes)
4. Increase referral ratio (target 1 referral ask per 3 applications)
5. Focus applications on stack-matching roles only

---

- Full-Stack Engineer (Frontend-leaning)
- UI Engineer (Product Teams)

---

## Final Note

Consistency beats intensity. If you execute this plan daily for 30 days with strong portfolio positioning, your interview pipeline should improve significantly.
